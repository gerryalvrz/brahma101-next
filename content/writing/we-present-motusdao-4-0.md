**2022.** The theory. Our first whitepaper, the initial research, the metaverse congress. Exploring consciousness, complex systems, and universal healthcare.

**2025.** The substrate. Our first infrastructure experiments. Privacy, identity, payments, communication, and data architecture.

**2026.** The convergence. We are approaching the first complete implementation of our original vision. The Academy becomes another layer of the ecosystem, making education accessible while creating economic value.

**2027.** The stress test. Test the infrastructure, grow the network, strengthen the economic model, and raise capital.

**2028.** Universal Mental Income.

And from there on?

## Web 4.0

We now declare the beginning of the Web4 era.

For us, Web4 represents the natural evolution from an internet of isolated interfaces to an internet of coordinated intelligence.

From navigating platforms to expressing intent.

From manually operating software to autonomous agents acting on our behalf.

Code on demand dissolves technical barriers. Intelligent systems coordinate through emerging protocols. The internet becomes a unified point of action.

But there is a catch.

Who owns the agents?

Who controls their memory?

Who holds their keys?

Who captures the value?

If we replace applications with autonomous systems controlled by the same corporations, we have changed the interface without changing the power structure.

A more intelligent cage is still a cage.

**Web4 cannot merely make machines more autonomous. It must make humans more autonomous.**
